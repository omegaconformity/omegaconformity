How we work

Built around the technical evidence.

We assess products using relevant technical information, applicable standards, physical observations and available test evidence. Each assignment is approached according to its purpose and scope.

  • Defined scope and relevant technical requirements
  • Review of drawings, manuals, marking and technical files
  • Inspection and testing arrangements where appropriate
  • Clear technical observations and reporting
