What we do

Testing, inspection &
technical assessment.

From initial technical review through inspection, testing arrangements and reporting, OMEGA provides focused support for conformity assessment projects.

Inspection & On-Site Assessment

Technical and physical assessment activities conducted at production, installation or shipment stages.

Pre-shipment inspection · Visual and dimensional examination · Installation and site checks · Technical condition reporting

CE Compliance & Technical Documentation

Support with identifying applicable CE requirements and preparing or reviewing technical documentation, including Technical Construction Files (TCF).

Technical file review · EU declaration review · Product marking · Instructions and technical information

Testing & Laboratory Coordination

Test planning, sample and documentation coordination, and review of available test evidence. Activities can be arranged with specialized external laboratories.

EMC testing coordination · Electrical safety · Mechanical tests · Applicable standard-based assessments

Risk Assessment & Machinery Safety

Structured identification and assessment of hazards and safety-related information for machinery and other industrial equipment.

EN ISO 12100 methodology · Safety measures review · Hazard identification · Risk assessment documentation

Technical Reporting & Document Review

Technical assessments documented through reports, checklists, findings and supporting annexes appropriate to the defined assignment.

Inspection reports · Technical review reports · Documentation evaluations · Supporting evidence records

Certification Process Support

Technical preparation and coordination for relevant certification and conformity assessment routes, including communication with competent notified bodies where third-party assessment is necessary.

Scope review · Document readiness · Coordination with external bodies · Project follow-up
