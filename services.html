What we do

Testing, inspection &
technical assessment.

OMEGA provides on-site machinery inspection, applicable safety testing, risk assessment and technical file support, including work at manufacturing facilities and installation sites. We also assist with inspection certificates and certification processes.

Factory & On-Site Inspection

OMEGA technical specialists assess machinery directly at manufacturing facilities, installation sites and other locations where the equipment is available. Inspection activities can include visual and dimensional checks, safety-related observations, measurements and technical inspection reporting.

Factory visits · Pre-shipment inspection · Visual and dimensional checks · On-site inspection reports

On-Site Testing & TCF Assistance

Our specialists perform on-site machinery inspections and applicable technical tests at manufacturing facilities, installation sites and other equipment locations. Depending on the machine and agreed assessment scope, these may include functional and safety checks, electrical measurements, mechanical inspections and verification against relevant technical standards. Results support risk assessment, technical reporting and preparation or review of Technical Construction Files (TCF).

On-site checks and tests · TCF preparation support · Technical evidence collection · Safety reviews

CE Compliance & Technical Documentation

We help identify the EU requirements and technical standards that apply to a product. We also prepare or review technical files, declarations, manuals, product marking and related documentation.

EU legislation and standards · Technical files · Declarations · Nameplates and instructions

Testing & Laboratory Coordination

Where technically appropriate, our specialists carry out machinery checks and measurements on site. Tests requiring dedicated laboratory facilities are coordinated with cooperating specialist laboratories; the resulting reports can be included in the technical assessment.

EMC testing · Electrical safety · Machinery measurements · Laboratory coordination

Risk Assessment & Machinery Safety

We review machinery hazards, safety measures and technical information to support structured risk assessment and safety documentation.

EN ISO 12100 · Hazard identification · Risk reduction measures · Risk assessment documentation

Inspection Certificates & Technical Reports

For defined inspection and technical assessment projects, OMEGA can issue its own Certificates of Inspection, inspection reports and technical review reports. Records of Omega-issued documents can be checked through our online verification service.

Certificates of Inspection · Inspection reports · Technical review reports · Document verification

Certification & Notified Body Coordination

We support manufacturers with technical preparation for certification and conformity assessment. Where third-party assessment is required, we can help coordinate the process with the relevant testing laboratories and notified bodies.

Certification route review · Document preparation · Notified body liaison · Project follow-up
