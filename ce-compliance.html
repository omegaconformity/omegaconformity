Understanding which EU product rules apply is an essential part of preparing a product for the European market. We provide technical support with the assessment and documentation process.

Regulatory overview

From applicable requirements to technical evidence.

CE marking indicates that a manufacturer declares a product’s conformity with applicable EU harmonization legislation. The precise route depends on the product and the relevant legislation.

  • Identify applicable directives, regulations and technical standards.
  • Determine the relevant conformity assessment procedure.
  • Review technical documentation, risk assessment and test evidence.
  • Support marking, declarations and documentation readiness.

Selected EU legislation

Common areas of technical assessment.

Applicable rules depend on product characteristics, intended use and placing on the market.

2006/42/EC

Machinery Directive

Machinery design, safety requirements, risk assessment and technical documentation.

2014/30/EU

EMC Directive

Electromagnetic compatibility requirements for electrical and electronic equipment.

2014/35/EU

Low Voltage Directive

Electrical equipment safety within the Directive’s specified voltage limits.

2014/53/EU

Radio Equipment Directive

Essential requirements for products with relevant radio functionality.

2000/14/EC

Outdoor Noise Directive

Noise emission requirements for certain equipment intended for outdoor use.

2023/1230

EU Machinery Regulation

The EU Machinery Regulation replaces the Machinery Directive in 2027, with applicable transition provisions.

Conformity assessment routes vary. Some products permit manufacturer self-assessment; others require a designated third party. Where needed, notified body activities are addressed through the appropriately designated organization.
Documentation essentials

What a technical file may include

The extent of technical documentation is determined by the legislation applicable to the product and the chosen assessment procedure.

01 / PRODUCT INFORMATION

Product description & drawings

General descriptions, design information, engineering drawings, component and system details.

02 / SAFETY

Risk assessment

Identification of hazards, safety-related decisions and supporting risk reduction information.

03 / TECHNICAL EVIDENCE

Test & evaluation records

Applicable standards, available test reports and evidence used within the assessment.

04 / PRODUCT DOCUMENTS

Marking & instructions

Relevant labels, user information, declarations and supporting technical documentation.
